FAQ

Good questions.

Will it still look like me?

That's the point. Our looks are written to keep your face, skin tone and body exactly as they are. If a result doesn't look like you, retry it for free.

Do you keep my photos?

No. Photos you send for an AI edit aren't stored by us, results are deleted from our servers as soon as your device has them (within an hour at most), and nothing is ever used to train AI. Effects, video edits, background removal and adjustments never leave your iPhone.

Is it free?

Yes, to start. Retro effects, video editing, background removal, adjustments and Best shot are free and run right on your device. You also get 3 free AI credits every day. Pro adds 100 credits a month, our best-quality AI, every trend and no watermark.

Can I edit videos?

Yes, for free. Cut out the silences like a jump cut, add an 80s or 90s effect and trim, then save or share. Clips can be up to 5 minutes, with no watermark and no ending clip.

Which languages and devices?

Glowlit works on iPhone and iPad and speaks 12 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Indonesian. It follows your iPhone's language, and you can pick another one just for Glowlit in the app's Settings › Language.

Are there widgets?

Yes. Add Today's trend or My glow to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, and follow your glow-up in the Dynamic Island while the AI works, even after you leave the app.

How old do I need to be?

Glowlit is for ages 13 and up. If you're under 18, talk to a parent or guardian before buying anything or turning on cloud AI.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. We also remind you the day before a free trial ends.
