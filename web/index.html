FAQ

Good questions.

Will it still look like me?

That's the point. Our looks are written to keep your face, skin tone and body exactly as they are. If a result doesn't look like you, retry it for free.

Do you keep my photos?

No. Photos you send for an AI edit aren't stored by us, results are deleted from our servers within an hour, and nothing is ever used to train AI.

Is it free?

You get 3 free credits every day, and background removal and adjustments are always free. Pro adds 100 credits a month, every trend and no watermark.

Are there widgets?

Yes. Add Today's trend or My glow to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, and follow your glow-up in the Dynamic Island while the AI works, even after you leave the app.

How old do I need to be?

Glowlit is for ages 13 and up. If you're under 18, talk to a parent or guardian before buying anything or turning on cloud AI.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. We also remind you the day before a free trial ends.
