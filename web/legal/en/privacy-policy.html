2. Designed with teens in mind

Glowlit is designed with teen users in mind. That's why we apply high-privacy defaults to everyone who uses it, whatever their age:

  • No ads. We don't show advertising, and we don't use advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking. We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, the only third-party analytics SDK is RevenueCat's subscription analytics, which starts only once you make a purchase (see section 5), and we don't access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No profiling. We don't build profiles about you, and we don't make decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects based on automated processing.
  • No public profiles or feeds. Nothing about you is public in Glowlit. Your creations are shared only if you choose to share them.
  • No sharing for marketing. We don't sell personal information or share it for advertising or marketing, and we don't work with data brokers. We don't send you marketing messages.
  • Your library stays on your device. The creations you keep are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Cloud processing only after you opt in. Nothing leaves your device for one of our cloud AI features until you agree. Apple's Image Playground has its own notice (section 6).
  • No AI training. We never use your photos, prompts or results to train AI models, and we use only AI provider plans whose terms don't allow training on customer content without our permission, which we never give.
  • No face recognition. We don't perform face recognition, create biometric templates or face embeddings, or try to identify the people in your photos.

We follow the standards of the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the "Children's Code") for all users. Section 18 explains how.

3. If you're under 18

This section is for you if you're under 18. The rest of this policy applies to you too, but these are the most important points in plain words.

  • What we know about you. Very little. We don't have your name, email address, phone number, password or location. Your account is just a random ID.
  • Your photos. Your creations stay on your phone. If you use a cloud AI feature, the photo and text you choose are sent to our servers and AI partners only to make your image.   • Ask first. Talk to your parent or guardian before you switch on cloud AI processing or buy anything. Under 18, the paywall never opens by itself. If you're under 13, you can use the free tools that run on your device, and the app sends nothing to us.
  • A parent or guardian step. If you're 13 to 15, or under the age of digital consent where you live, the cloud AI consent screen asks you to read it with a parent or guardian and to confirm that they said yes. We can't check this answer, so please be honest.
  • Purchases. Purchases are charged to the Apple Account on your device. If you have a child account in Family Sharing, your parent or guardian may need to approve purchases with "Ask to Buy".

  • You're in control. You can switch off cloud AI processing in Settings at any time, and delete everything with Settings → Delete my data & account.
  • Questions or worries? Talk to a trusted adult. You, your parent or your guardian can email us at privacy@glowlit.app.

4. Data that stays on your iPhone

Much of Glowlit works without sending anything to us. The following data is stored only on your device, and we can't access it:

  • Your library ("My Works"): the images you keep, with their title, the type of edit, the text prompt you used (for Create from text and Magic Edit), whether you marked them as a favourite, and timestamps. The library is stored in the app's private storage and protected by iOS Data Protection.
  • App settings (stored in iOS UserDefaults): whether you've completed onboarding; the interests you picked (for example "Selfie glow-ups" or "Shots for my shop"), which are used only to order the home screen on your device; your age range (under 13, 13 to 15, 16 to 17, or 18 and over), whether it came from Apple's Declared Age Range or the app's own question, and when it was set (never your birth date); your cloud AI consent choice, when you made it, which version of the consent screen and which processors it named (so we can ask again when they change) and, where asked, your answer to "A parent or guardian said yes"; your appearance preference; your notification choices; a small count of happy moments (saving, sharing or hearting a creation) and when the app last asked, used only to time Apple's rating prompt; and, only in offline or demo mode, a local credit balance.
  • Session tokens for your anonymous account, stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Reminders: notifications are off until you allow them and switch them on. The app then schedules them locally on your device: a reminder before the cancel-by time of a free trial (at least 26 hours before it ends, earlier if that would fall at night or in school hours); "your edit is ready" when a creation finishes while you're in another app (skipped at night and in school hours; it never quotes your prompt); "your free credits are back" (only after you run out); and, if you choose, a look of the week. Reminders are never sent between 9 pm and 9 am, or during school hours (8 am to 3 pm) on weekdays. No push server or device token is involved.
  • Widgets and Live Activity: to show your credits, your latest creation and its progress on the Home Screen, the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, the app shares a small snapshot (credit balance, the title and a small thumbnail of your latest creation, and how many creations you have) with its widgets through a container on your device. It never leaves your iPhone.

On-device features. Remove background (using Apple's Vision framework, including picking which subject to keep, new backgrounds and stickers), Hide faces, Adjust, Effects (including the Digicam '04 look offered during onboarding), Retro Cam and Photo Booth process your photos entirely on your iPhone.

On-device face checks. These features look at faces, only on your iPhone, with Apple's Vision framework: Best shot ranks the selfies you pick by light, focus and how clearly the face shows; the kindness reminder counts how many people are in a photo; the likeness check compares an AI edit with your original photo (face proportions and skin lightness) so it can offer a free retry if the result drifted; Hide faces finds where faces are so you can blur or cover other people's faces before you share; and the Cover star and Photo strip share designs and Photo Booth prints find where the face is so the crop keeps it in frame. Nothing is stored or sent to us, nobody is identified or recognised, and nothing rates anyone's looks.

On-device writing help. On devices with Apple Intelligence, the idea and prompt suggestions in Create ("Inspire me" and "Make it magic") may be written by Apple's on-device language model. Your text and the suggestions stay on your device and are not sent to us or to Apple.

Photo access. You pick photos with Apple's system photo picker, so Glowlit can see only the photos you choose, not your whole library. To save results to your Photos library, the app asks for add-only permission, which lets it add images but not read your library.

Camera access. Retro Cam and Photo Booth use your camera only while they're open, after you allow camera access. The live preview and the effects run on your iPhone, and your shots stay on your device until you save or share them.

Backups. Data stored by the app may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup), depending on your settings. Apple handles backups under its own terms.

You can delete your library in Settings → Delete all creations. It's also deleted when you delete your account or the app.

5. Data we process on our servers

Anonymous account

When you first use a cloud AI feature or make a purchase, the app creates an anonymous account with our backend provider (Supabase). The account is identified by a random user ID. We don't collect your name, email address, phone number or password.

Optional account (Sign in with Apple)

Signing in is optional. If you choose Sign in with Apple (offered so Pro and your credits follow you to another iPhone), Apple confirms your identity and our backend provider receives a stable Apple user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address, which can be a private relay address that forwards to you. We don't ask for your name and never see your Apple ID password. The anonymous account you used on that iPhone is merged into your account: paid credits, subscription records, job records and reports move over, and the anonymous account is deleted. We also keep a token from Apple so that, when you delete your account, we can revoke Glowlit's access to your Apple ID, as Apple requires. Your photos and library are not synced and stay on each iPhone.

Content you send to cloud AI features

When you use Magic Edit, Styles, AI Enhance, Erase or Create from text, the app sends the content needed for that request to our backend, which forwards it to our AI provider. This means your photo, the mask you brushed (for Erase) and your text prompt (for Create from text and Magic Edit). Section 6 explains what happens to it.

Job records

For each cloud AI job, we keep a record with: a job ID, your anonymous user ID, the type of operation, the template ID, the name of the AI model endpoint, the credit cost, the status, any error code and message, retry flags, the provider's request ID and timestamps. Job records don't contain the photo you sent or your prompt. Until you download them, they hold your results, which are deleted within 1 hour. We also use them to apply rate limits (6 jobs per minute on the free tier and 20 with Pro).

Credits

Your credit wallet (daily, subscription and purchased balances, with the related period dates) and your credit ledger (holds and refunds for each job, and credit pack grants with the Apple transaction ID).

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes all payments. We never receive your card details, your name or your Apple Account email address.

To verify purchases, we receive Apple-signed transaction data, including transaction IDs, the product ID, purchase and expiry dates, the environment (sandbox or production) and an app account token that equals your anonymous user ID. Apple keeps this token with its purchase records under Apple's own privacy policy.

For subscriptions, we keep an entitlement record: the Apple original transaction ID, the product ID, the expiry date, a revoked flag and the environment, linked to your anonymous user ID.

To understand how our subscriptions perform (trials, renewals, cancellations, refunds), once you make a purchase (or if your Apple Account already has purchases with us) the app also shares the same purchase records with our subscription analytics provider, RevenueCat, together with your anonymous user ID and basic device and app information (app version, iOS version, country and language). RevenueCat never receives your photos, prompts or results.

Reports

If you use "Report this image", we receive the reason you select, any details you choose to add, the related job ID, your anonymous user ID and timestamps.

Server and security logs

Our hosting provider records technical logs when the app connects to our servers, such as IP address, request metadata and timestamps.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, the content of your message and anything you attach.

6. How cloud AI processing works

  1. Your consent comes first. Before any photo or prompt leaves your device for one of our cloud AI features, the app shows an opt-in screen that names the companies that process it. Nothing is sent until you agree. If you're under the age required by law, a parent or guardian must give or approve this consent (sections 3, 17 and 19). You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Cloud AI processing, and on-device features keep working.
  2. Upload. The app sends your input over an encrypted connection to our backend (Supabase Edge Functions; hosting region: [Supabase region, e.g. EU (Frankfurt)]).
  3. Safety checks. Your prompt is checked by our safety filters before the request is processed (section 9).
  4. AI processing. Our backend passes your input to our AI inference provider for that request only. By default, this is xAI (X.AI LLC and its affiliates, USA), which runs its Grok Imagine image models. If we change providers, jobs may instead go to fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc., USA), which runs third-party models (where fal.ai uses a model developer's own service to run a model, for example Google for its Gemini image models, that developer also processes the request), or directly to Google (Gemini API) or OpenAI (Images API). The current list is in section 12.
  5. No storage of inputs. We don't store your photo, mask or prompt. We use xAI's Zero Data Retention option, under which xAI deletes requests and results within 1 hour and keeps no stored copies. When fal.ai is used, we instruct it not to keep request history, which means no storage of inputs or outputs.
  6. Delivery. The result is checked by automated safety filters and held temporarily in our database or on the provider's content delivery network, only so the app can download it. It is deleted as soon as the app confirms the download, and after 1 hour at the latest.
  7. On your device. If you keep the result, it's saved to your library on your iPhone.

We use only provider plans whose terms don't allow using customer content to train their models without our permission, and we never give that permission. We never use your photos, prompts or results to train AI models, sell them, or use them for advertising, marketing or data mining.

Create with Image Playground (Apple). On devices that support it, Create also offers Create with Image Playground, Apple's own image maker. It's Apple's feature, not one of our cloud AI features, so our consent screen and credits don't apply. Your description goes to Apple's Image Playground, which makes the image on your device or, from iOS 27, on Apple's Private Cloud Compute servers, under Apple's terms and with Apple's own usage limits; on those versions the app tells you before the first use. The app allows only Apple's illustration styles and turns off the option to use people from your photo library. Glowlit receives only the finished image, which is kept in your library on your device and is never sent to us.

9. Safety filters and automated decisions

To keep Glowlit safe, we use automated safety measures:

  • prompt filters in the app and on our servers that block, for example, nudity and sexual content, "undressing" or nudification requests, and any sexual content involving minors;
  • our AI providers' safety settings; and
  • an automated check of every output for nudity or sexual content.

If a filter blocks a request or a result, you aren't charged, and any credits are refunded.

These filters are the only automated decisions we make about your requests. They don't have legal or similarly significant effects on you within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR, and we don't profile you. If you think a filter blocked something by mistake, contact us at support@glowlit.app and a human will review it.

14. How long we keep data

  • Photos, masks and prompts sent to cloud AI features: not stored by us. They are passed to the AI provider for that request only.

  • Job records: 30 days, then deleted.
  • Credit wallet and credit ledger: as long as your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Entitlement records: as long as your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account.
  • Anonymous account (user ID): as long as your account exists. Deleted when you delete your account, or when it's merged into your account after Sign in with Apple.

  • Server and security logs: a short period, typically no more than 90 days, for security and abuse prevention. They are deleted on this schedule even after you delete your account.
  • Emails and privacy requests: as long as needed to handle your request and to document how we handled it, unless we must keep them longer to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it, delete your account or delete the app.

We may keep data for longer where the law requires it or where it's needed for legal claims, for example information we must preserve when we report illegal content to the authorities.

15. How we protect your data

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) for connections between the app, our servers and our providers.
  • Row-level security in our database, so that each account can read only its own records, with all changes made by our server functions.
  • Session tokens stored in the iOS Keychain, and your library protected by iOS Data Protection.
  • AI provider keys and other secrets kept only on our servers, never in the app.
  • Least-privilege access to our systems and data.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we'll notify the authorities and you where the law requires it.

18. How we apply the UK Children's Code

The UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the "Children's Code") sets 15 standards for online services likely to be used by under-18s. We apply them to all users, wherever they live:

  • Best interests of the child. We put the best interests of young users first when we design features. For example, we block sexual content for everyone, we don't offer body-reshaping or skin-lightening features, and we have no public profiles or feeds.
  • Data protection impact assessments. We assess the risks our features pose to young users, including through data protection impact assessments, before we launch them.

Under 18, the paywall never opens by itself, and where the law asks for it the consent screen includes a parent or guardian step.   • Transparency. Each of our documents starts with a short summary, this policy has a plain-language section for under-18s (section 3), and the cloud AI consent screen explains in plain words what's sent and to whom before anything is sent.

  • Policies and community standards. We enforce our Terms of Use and AUP, using safety filters, reporting and human review of appeals.
  • Default settings. High privacy is the default: cloud AI processing is off until you opt in, your library stays on your device, nothing is public, and the app sends notifications only if you allow them.
  • Data minimisation. We collect only what each feature you use needs. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or location.

  • Geolocation. We don't collect or use your location.
  • Parental controls. Glowlit has no tools that let parents or anyone else monitor your activity or location. Parents can use Apple's Family Sharing and Screen Time, which are provided by Apple.
  • Profiling. We don't profile users.
  • Nudge techniques. We don't use design tricks to get you to share more data or to switch off privacy protections. You can choose "Not now" on the cloud AI consent screen and keep using on-device features.
  • Connected toys and devices. Not applicable: Glowlit isn't a connected toy or device.
