{{APP_NAME}} is designed with teen users in mind. That's why we apply high-privacy defaults to everyone who uses it, whatever their age:

- **No public profiles or feeds.** Nothing about you is public in {{APP_NAME}}. Your creations are shared only if you choose to share them.
- **No sharing for marketing.** We don't sell personal information or share it for personalised (cross-context behavioural) advertising or for marketing, and we don't work with data brokers. We don't send you marketing messages.
- **Your library stays on your device.** The creations you keep are stored only on your device.
- **Cloud processing only after you opt in.** Nothing leaves your device for one of our cloud AI features until you agree. Apple's Image Playground has its own notice (section 6).
- **No AI training.** We never use your photos, prompts or results to train AI models, and we use only AI provider plans whose terms don't allow training on customer content without our permission, which we never give.

We follow the standards of the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the "Children's Code") for all users. Section 18 explains how.

### Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes all payments. We never receive your card details, and purchases don't give us your name or email address.

To verify purchases, we receive Apple-signed transaction data, including transaction IDs, the product ID, purchase and expiry dates, the environment (sandbox or production) and an app account token that equals your anonymous user ID. For subscriptions, we keep an entitlement record: the Apple original transaction ID, the product ID, the expiry date, a revoked flag (set when Apple refunds or revokes the subscription), the environment, whether the current period is a free trial, and the subscription's credit balance with its period dates, linked to your anonymous user ID.

**Refunds and revocations.** Apple sends our server signed notifications about {{APP_NAME}} purchases (App Store Server Notifications), including when Apple refunds a purchase, revokes it (for example when Family Sharing access to it ends) or reverses a refund. ## 6. How cloud AI processing works

1. **Your consent comes first.** Before any photo or prompt leaves your device for one of our cloud AI features, the app shows an opt-in screen that names the companies that process it. Nothing is sent until you agree. If you're under the age required by law, a parent or guardian must give or approve this consent (sections 3, 17 and 19). You can withdraw consent at any time in **Settings → Cloud AI processing**, and on-device features keep working.
2. **Upload.** The app sends your input over an encrypted connection to our backend (Supabase Edge Functions; hosting region: {{HOSTING_REGION}}).
3. **Safety checks.** Your prompt is checked by our safety filters before the request is processed. If you're editing a photo, our independent safety check also looks at the photo before the image model receives it: our backend sends the photo, never your prompt or mask, to Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC, USA), whose Claude model checks it. A photo it flags, or can't check, isn't edited, and your credit is refunded (section 9).
4. **AI processing.** Our backend passes your input to our AI inference provider for that request only. By default, this is xAI (X.AI LLC and its affiliates, USA), which runs its Grok Imagine image models. With each request, our backend also gives xAI a pseudonymous user tag so that xAI can detect abuse: a shortened one-way hash of your random user ID, the same for all your requests. It isn't your name or email address, and xAI can't work out your user ID from it. If we change providers, jobs may instead go to fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc., USA), which runs third-party models (where fal.ai uses a model developer's own service to run a model, for example Google for its Gemini image models, that developer also processes the request), or directly to Google (Gemini API) or OpenAI (Images API). The current list is in section 12.
5. **We don't store your inputs.** We don't store your photo, mask or prompt. We use xAI's Zero Data Retention option, under which xAI deletes requests and results within 1 hour and keeps no stored copies. Anthropic deletes the photos and results it checks within 30 days of receipt, unless its automated systems flag them for a breach of its Usage Policy, in which case it may keep them for up to 2 years, or the law requires it to keep them longer. For content it flags, it may keep its trust and safety classification scores (not the content itself) for up to 7 years. When fal.ai is used, we instruct it not to keep request history, which means no storage of inputs or outputs.
6. **Delivery.** The result is checked by automated safety filters, including our independent safety check, and held temporarily in our database or on the provider's content delivery network, only so the app can download it. It is deleted as soon as the app confirms the download, and after 1 hour at the latest.
7. **On your device.** If you keep the result, it's saved to your library on your device.

We use only provider plans whose terms don't allow using customer content to train their models without our permission, and we never give that permission. We never use your photos, prompts or results to train AI models, sell them, or use them for advertising, marketing or data mining.

**Create with Image Playground (Apple).** On devices that support it, Create also offers **Create with Image Playground**, Apple's own image maker. It's Apple's feature, not one of our cloud AI features, so our consent screen and credits don't apply. Your description goes to Apple's Image Playground, which makes the image on your device or, from iOS 27 (macOS 27 on a Mac), on Apple's Private Cloud Compute servers, under Apple's terms and with Apple's own usage limits; on those versions the app tells you before the first use. The app allows only Apple's illustration styles and turns off the option to use people from your photo library. {{APP_NAME}} receives only the finished image, which is kept in your library on your device and is never sent to us.
