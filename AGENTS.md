## 1. The product

**Glowlit — "Make it glow."** An iPhone and iPad app for girls and young women (13+), also built for the Mac with Mac Catalyst: AI photo editing and image creation, plus free on-device tools.

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Magic Edit** | "Say the change" ("give me pink hair"), then compare before/after; one free retry if the result doesn't look like you (Likeness Lock + an on-device likeness check). "Ideas for this photo" under the canvas (picked on device from what the photo shows) and "Surprise me" (free effects). The prompt policy blocks sexual, revealing, humiliating and body/face/skin-reshaping requests in all 12 languages, and every edit keeps faces, bodies, skin tone, skin texture and age |
| **One-tap looks** | 51 year-round templates (Doll Box, Y2K Digicam, 80s Studio Portrait, Plushie Me, Bow Era, Sticker Sheet, pet portraits…) in 8 collections, including Bestie pics (Sleepover, Bracelet Club, Twinning, Concert Night), plus seasonal looks (Halloween Oct 1–Nov 2, winter Dec 1–Jan 6, Galentine's Feb 1–14) that Home promotes only in season and the editor keeps all year (`SeasonalTheme`; DEBUG `-GLDate yyyy-MM-dd` fakes the day) |
| **Create** | Text to image with 20 styles, 5 sizes, up to 4 images with Pro; "Inspire me" and "Make it magic" written on the device by Apple Intelligence when available; "Create with Image Playground" (Apple's image maker, iOS 18.4+ on supported devices); new results "develop" like an instant photo |
| **Free on-device tools** | Twelve retro effects from the 80s to the 2010s (incl. Camcorder '06 with a running timecode on video, Film Still with letterbox and a subtitle, Toy Cam '09's cross-processed colour and Rainbow '17's prism light) plus the Tiny World tilt-shift, Adjust with one-tap Auto, Crop (posting shapes, rotate, mirror, straighten), RAW auto-develop, Cut out (tap to pick the subject; a Keep more / Keep less refine brush; colour, gradient, blurred-original or own-photo backgrounds, with an optional soft shadow; outlined stickers), Hide faces (blur, pixelate or cover other people's faces, with a "Hide their faces" button on the kindness reminder, and "Hide other faces" in the share composer for photos from My Works or Create), Text & Stickers (text in six fonts and four styles, a sticker pack drawn in code, pasted cut-out stickers, PencilKit doodles; editable until Apply), Best shot (finds the selfie with the best light and focus), **Collage** (photo dumps from 2–9 photos and seamless 2–5-slide carousels), simple **video editor** (auto-cut silences, retro effects, trim, automatic **captions** made on the device in five styles), **Retro Cam** (the effects live in the camera, self-timer, flash; **Photo Booth**: 4 shots with a 3-2-1 countdown, printed as a strip or a 2×2 grid with a caption and the date) |
| **Sharing** | Photo, Before/After, 9:16 Story card, **Cover star** magazine cover, **Photo strip**, Which vibe? (two variations of one creation, never her original against an edit); caption ideas about the vibe, never looks (on-device writer or a curated list); Instagram Stories via the share sheet (no Meta App ID needed); "Glow together" invite card with a QR code |
| **Engagement** | Home/Lock Screen widgets, Dynamic Island Live Activity, iOS 18 Control Center controls, Siri / Shortcuts / Spotlight / Action button for the free tools, one-time tips for new features, gentle local notifications (never at night or during school hours, never showing her prompt), a first run that ends with a real on-device creation, then the guided tour; a Feel good page in Settings. No feeds, likes or streaks |
| **Accounts** | No account until the first consented cloud job or purchase (RevenueCat only for buyers); optional Sign in with Apple so Pro and credits follow the user; Delete my data removes everything, RevenueCat included |
| **Age** | A neutral age step in onboarding (Apple's Declared Age Range on iOS 26+, otherwise month and year menus), stored on the device only. Under 13: on-device tools only, and no ads. Under 18: no automatic paywall, and a guardian step in the consent sheet for 13–15 and below the storefront's digital-consent age |
| **Money** | Free: 3 AI credits/day, plus "Watch a short ad · +1 credit" when out of credits (≤ 3 a day; the credit comes only from AdMob's signed server callback) and an occasional interstitial after a finished save or share. Pro Yearly $39.99 (7-day trial), Pro Monthly $5.99 (first month $1.99), packs 30/$2.99 and 100/$7.99; Pro has no ads. No weekly plans. Real balances everywhere (a tap on one explains credits, never a paywall), the paywall says why it opened and what stays free, packs offered when Pro runs out, billed terms beside the trial button, Ask to Buy supported |
| **Privacy** | Explicit, versioned consent naming every processor before any cloud AI; photos never train AI; results deleted after download (≤ 1 hour); xAI with Zero Data Retention; IPTC "made with AI" metadata on exports that survives re-edits (`Provenance`), with no AI label or mark on on-device edits; the on-device writer keeps text on the device; ads (Google AdMob, free members 13+) are never personalised, never get photos, prompts or the user id, and the app never asks for tracking (no ATT, no IDFA) |
| **Languages** | English + Turkish, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Indonesian |
| **Mac** | The same app on macOS 14+ (Mac Catalyst, iPad interface scaled to the Mac): menu bar (⌘N New Creation, ⌘O Open Photo…, ⌘1–⌘4 tabs, ⌘, Settings, Help › Contact support), drop a photo file on the window to edit it, Esc and ⌘S in the editors, Retro Cam with the Mac's camera (hidden without one; Space takes the photo), one resizable window. Not on the Mac: the Live Activity, Instagram Stories sharing and ads (Google's ad SDK doesn't support Mac Catalyst) |

Brand: "Blush" palette (blush `#F7C1D9`, petal `#F8E0EA`, ink `#121212`) for UI; colorful "Aura" accents only for the icon, share cards and template art. Fonts: Poppins + New York Italic accent words. Details: [`docs/02-brand-identity.md`](docs/02-brand-identity.md).

## 3. Build, run, test

```bash
# App in the simulator (pass an explicit simulator id)
xcodebuild build -project ios/Glowlit.xcodeproj -scheme Glowlit -configuration Debug \
  -destination 'id=<simulator-udid>' -quiet

# CI-style device build without signing
xcodebuild build -project ios/Glowlit.xcodeproj -scheme Glowlit -configuration Release \
  -destination 'generic/platform=iOS' CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO -quiet

# Mac Catalyst build without signing (also in CI)
xcodebuild build -project ios/Glowlit.xcodeproj -scheme Glowlit -configuration Debug \
  -destination 'generic/platform=macOS,variant=Mac Catalyst' CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO -quiet

# AppCore unit tests (also run on Linux in CI)
swift test --package-path ios/Packages/AppCore

# Backend: Deno checks (CI) and SQL tests on a throwaway Postgres
(cd backend/supabase/functions && deno fmt --check && deno lint && deno check ai-image/index.ts credits-redeem/index.ts account/index.ts report/index.ts ad-reward/index.ts && deno test _shared/)
backend/scripts/test-db.sh

# Generated files must stay in sync (CI checks both)
python3 scripts/build-legal.py --check
python3 scripts/export-core-strings.py --check
python3 scripts/check-honest-copy.py      # no unsupported claims, device-neutral app copy (CI)
python3 scripts/check-release-config.py   # release blockers: demo mode, legal placeholders, website URL
                                          # (CI runs it with --report; upload-app-store.sh with --online)

# Archive and upload to App Store Connect / TestFlight with an API key
# (key file in ~/.appstoreconnect/private_keys/; ids from Users and Access → Integrations)
# It stops on release blockers; GL_PREVIEW_BUILD=1 allows a deliberate preview (never submit it for review)
ASC_KEY_ID=<key id> ASC_ISSUER_ID=<issuer id> scripts/upload-app-store.sh
PLATFORM=mac ASC_KEY_ID=<key id> ASC_ISSUER_ID=<issuer id> scripts/upload-app-store.sh   # Mac App Store
```

Running the Mac app without an Apple account in Xcode: sign ad hoc (`CODE_SIGN_IDENTITY=- CODE_SIGNING_REQUIRED=NO`) and point `CODE_SIGN_ENTITLEMENTS` at a scratch copy of `Config/Glowlit-macOS.entitlements` without the App Group and Sign in with Apple keys (they need a provisioning profile; never commit that). Keep the App Sandbox on: unsandboxed, SwiftData would use `~/Library/Application Support/default.store`, which other apps can share. Screenshot scenes work there too: `open <…>/Debug-maccatalyst/Glowlit.app --args -GLScreenshotScene home`.

Deterministic screens for QA and marketing (DEBUG builds): launch with `-GLScreenshotScene <scene>` (see `ScreenshotScenes.swift`; e.g. `home`, `editor`, `effects`, `share -GLShareFormat cover`, `tour`, `invite`, `video -GLEffect camcorder06` on a generated clip, `camera -GLCameraAction booth` on demo art), and `-AppleLanguages "(tr)" -AppleLocale tr` for another language; `-GLPrompt <text>` sets Create's prompt in that language. On the Mac add `-GLWindowSize 1600x1000` (UIKit points; a 1232×770 window) so every capture has the same size, and capture the window by id (`screencapture -x -o -l <CGWindowID>`). `scripts/marketing-images.py` turns captures into App Store images (iPhone 1290×2796, iPad 2064×2752, Mac 2880×1800).

## 4. How things work

- **AI modes** (`AppConfig.aiMode`): *backend* (production: Supabase Edge Functions → xAI `grok-imagine-image-2.0`, low quality for free and medium for Pro, 1k), *direct* (DEBUG only, key from `ios/Config/Secrets.xcconfig`, `GL_AI_PROVIDER = xai|fal|openai|gemini`), *demo* (no backend: simulated results stamped "DEMO PREVIEW"; the banner says "Preview build", accounts and reports are hidden or say they aren't on yet, and a demo build installed from the App Store turns purchases off). Jobs are async: POST → poll → download → DELETE to confirm, which clears results server-side.
- **On-device writer** (`Services/AI/OnDeviceWriter.swift`, `app.writer`): Apple's Foundation Models (iOS 26+, Apple Intelligence, supported language) write Create's "Inspire me" ideas and "Make it magic" prompt upgrades, and can write edit ideas and captions (`ideas(_:)`). No consent or credits (nothing leaves the device); every line passes `ContentPolicy`; when the model is unavailable, callers fall back to `CuratedIdeas` and the offline `PromptBuilder.enhance`.
- **Create prompts and results:** `PromptHistory` (`app.prompts`) keeps the last 10 prompts that passed every gate and the draft in `UserDefaults.standard` only (never synced, never in the App Group), drops any that `ContentPolicy` blocks today, and is cleared by Delete my data; chips show while the box is empty. Results offer Regenerate (the same job through the same gates, after a cost confirm) and Save all. DEBUG: scene `recentPrompts`, `-GLKeepPrompts YES` for the stored ones.
- **Image Playground** (`Features/Create/ImagePlaygroundCreateButton.swift`, iOS 18.4+ and `supportsImagePlayground`): Apple's sheet with Apple's cartoon styles only, personalization off, no source image. It skips Glowlit's consent and credits (Apple's own feature, terms and limits); from iOS 27, where Apple may use Private Cloud Compute, a one-time Apple notice comes first. Results are saved with `Creation.source = imagePlayground`: no Glowlit mark, no report menu, no stored prompt, IPTC AI label on export.
- **Ideas for this photo** (`Features/Edit/EditorIdeasRow.swift`): Vision looks at the photo on device (`PhotoInsightsService`), and `LookSuggester` (AppCore, deterministic, never beauty looks or Glamour '92) picks a free effect, a look, a Magic Edit idea and a second effect. Which free look and which idea lead follows what the photo shows (`PhotoInsight.variety`, a stable hash of its labels), so different photos get different ones; an in-season look or idea always leads. Effects and "Surprise me" apply at once for free; a look asks first and goes through the usual gates; under 13 only effects are offered. Magic Edit idea texts use `idea("…")`, which `export-core-strings.py` picks up; their translations fill the prompt, so `TranslationTests` runs every translated idea and chip (found by translator comment) through `ContentPolicy`, and checks that every look, style and idea is translated in both catalogs. DEBUG: scene `ideas`, `-GLIdeasAction surprise`.
- **Age range** (`AgeBand`/`AgeDefaults` in AppCore, `AgeStepView`): asked once, before interests in onboarding (Apple's Declared Age Range on iOS 26+, otherwise neutral month and year menus); only the band, its source and date are stored, on the device. Under 13: on-device tools only (no Create tab, AI tools, paywall, account or RevenueCat). Under 18 and unknown: no automatic paywall, and the consent sheet's guardian step where `requiresGuardianStep`. A self-declared band can be raised only once a year. DEBUG: `-GLAgeBand <band>`, `-GLAgeMenus YES`, scene `age`.
- **Gates for every AI action:** age range → content policy (client + server, all app languages; checked before the consent sheet or paywall opens) → consent → Pro/credits. Credits are server-authoritative in production; failures are refunded exactly once.
- **Credits sheet and paywall reasons:** Explore's credit pill and the balance under Create's Generate open `CreditsSheet` (the balance, when free credits are back, what uses a credit, what is always free, one "Get more credits"), never the paywall. `presentPaywall(source:)` and `requestAccess(proSource:)` carry why the paywall opened (`out_of_credits`, `template_<id>`, `style_<id>`, `create_count`, `share_watermark`); `PaywallReason` turns that into the one-line card on top. Keep the list of free tools the same wherever it appears. DEBUG: `home -GLCreditsSheet YES`, `paywall -GLPaywallSource <source>`.
- **Consent record:** `AIConsentRecord` (AppCore) stores the consent version and the processor names the sheet showed; it goes stale when `consentVersion` or `aiProcessors` in `AppConfig.json` change, which asks again. List a new provider there before the backend uses it.
- **Notifications and the Lock Screen:** local only, timed by `NotificationPlanner` (never 21:00–09:00 or in weekday school hours; the trial reminder lands at least 26 h before the trial ends and follows `PurchaseManager.trial`). Notifications, the Live Activity and widget titles never show her prompt, only catalog names. The Friday look and trend widget use `TemplateCatalog.pushPool` (free looks unless Pro, no makeup looks). Under 13 the widget snapshot's `cloudAIAllowed` turns widgets and the Magic Edit control into free on-device tools.
- **First run:** onboarding ends with Digicam '04 on her own photo (on-device); Keep editing opens the editor, Save keeps it, and the adult soft paywall waits for that first success; the tour starts when a paywall, account prompt or editor closes.
- **On-device tools** never need consent or credits and save without a watermark. Free cloud AI results carry a small mark; Pro removes it.
- **Video captions** (`Features/Video/VideoCaptions.swift`, `CaptionsViews.swift`, `Services/Video/CaptionTranscriber.swift`, `CaptionRenderer.swift`, AppCore `Captions.swift`): speech is recognized on the device only (iOS 26: SpeechAnalyzer with SpeechTranscriber, else DictationTranscriber, models downloaded once through `AssetInventory`, no permission prompt; otherwise SFSpeechRecognizer with `requiresOnDeviceRecognition`, which asks for speech recognition permission). AppCore chunks words into cues (≤ 7 words, ≤ 3.5 s, two balanced lines of ~32 characters, CJK aware) kept in source time; `CaptionTimeline.remap` moves them through trim and silence cuts when the video is built. `CaptionTrack` draws each cue once per state (Pop, Bubble, Candy, Karaoke word, Typewriter), caches it, and composites it last in `VideoEngine.videoComposition`, so preview and export match and captions sit above the effect's date stamp, camcorder display or letterbox. DEBUG (scene `video`): `-GLCaptions fixture|auto|listening|nospeech|denied`, `-GLCaptionStyle`, `-GLCaptionsEditor YES`, `-GLVideoSeek <s>`, `-GLVideoSpeech YES` (a talking clip; real recognition works on the Mac build, the simulator has no speech models).
- **Text & Stickers** (`OverlaySession`, `TextStickersCanvas`, `OverlayRenderer`, `StickerArt`): items are placed with AppCore's resolution-independent `OverlayPlacement`, and one renderer draws both the canvas bitmaps and the full-size Apply. It's a manual edit (no AI marking); a pasted cut-out makes the photo a composite and keeps the sticker file's AI marking. Overlay text goes through `ContentPolicy` like the Film Still subtitle. Stickers are drawn in code, never emoji or SF Symbols in exports.
- **Provenance:** `Provenance` (AppCore) decides every export marking: the IPTC DigitalSourceType, the free mark, the AI hashtags and the "AI-edited" label. Pass it along whenever a result is re-opened (`openEditor(with:provenance:)`, `SharePayload.inheritedSourceType`, `Creation.provenance`); photos opened from files keep the marking they already carry (`PhotoDecoder` reads it). A Cut out placed on another photo sets `isComposite` (IPTC `composite`, not generative, so no AI label), which the Library keeps through `storedSourceType`. Export through `ImageExporter` / `PhotoLibrarySaver` only, never `pngData()` or `UIPasteboard.image` for AI content.
- **Collage** (`Features/Collage/`, from Explore's quick tools and the Edit tab; a full-screen cover): 2–9 picked photos as a grid, mosaic, film strip, instant prints, scrapbook or moodboard, in 4:5, 1:1, 9:16, 3:4 or a seamless carousel of 2–5 4:5 slides. Tapping the chosen style again shuffles its arrangements (`variantCount`), new ones appended so existing variant numbers keep their look: the mosaic's center stage, a garland of instant prints on ribbons, a tidy scrapbook page and a moodboard led by a featured photo. Layouts (normalized rects and rotations), per-photo pan and zoom (`CollageCrop`), carousel slicing (`CarouselSlicer`) and `Provenance.collage(of:)` (no marking unless a source carries one; then composite, or composite with AI) live in AppCore, so the live canvas and `CollageRenderer` (Core Graphics, off the main actor: 2160 px, slides 2160 × 2700 each, saved to Photos in order) draw the same picture. Free, on the device, no credits. DEBUG: scene `collage` with `-GLCollage…` arguments (`ScreenshotScenes.swift`), `-GLCollageExport YES` writes the export to tmp.
- **Localization:** String Catalogs. In SwiftUI use literals or `LocalizedStringKey`; in models use `String(localized:)`; AppCore text is English and translated at runtime with `L10n.core(_:)`, so after changing AppCore strings run `python3 scripts/export-core-strings.py`. Add or update translations with `xcodebuild -exportLocalizations` / `-importLocalizations`. Keep placeholders, `*accent*` pairs and line breaks identical. Effect names and their era tags (VHS '86, Tiny World, 80s, Y2K…) are marked "Don't translate": they read the same in every language, in the app and in Shortcuts. Legal documents exist in English and Turkish only.
- **Purchases:** StoreKit 2 in `PurchaseManager`; the backend verifies Apple-signed transactions. RevenueCat runs in observer mode for analytics only (project "Glowlit", public key in `AppConfig.json`). A purchase can come back *pending* (Ask to Buy, Texas SB 2420 parental consent): the paywall shows a neutral "Waiting for approval" state kept in memory only (StoreKit never reports a decline), and an approval arrives through `Transaction.updates` (`PurchaseManager.onLatePurchase`). To test it, select `ios/StoreKit/Products.storekit`, choose Editor › Enable Ask to Buy, then approve or decline in Debug › StoreKit › Manage Transactions; never commit the file with Ask to Buy switched on. Load failures are simulated with the file's StoreKit error settings (airplane mode has no effect on a local configuration).
- **Library (My Works):** SwiftData with a versioned schema (`CreationSchemaV1/V2/V3` + `GlowlitMigrationPlan` in `LibraryStore.swift`); never change a stored property of `Creation` without a new frozen schema version and a migration stage. Delete only sets `deletedAt`: the creation waits in Recently deleted for 30 days (`RecentlyDeleted` in AppCore) with Restore and Delete now, and `LibraryTrash.purgeExpired` removes due ones after launch (files off the main thread); queries and widgets skip deleted rows, and Delete all creations / Delete my data still remove everything. Select mode (bulk Share, Save, Favorite, Delete) and the tile context menu live in `LibraryView`; a tile whose file is gone offers Remove. DEBUG scenes `librarySelect`, `libraryTrash` with `-GLLibraryAction`, `-GLLibraryMissing YES`, and `-GLKeepLibrary YES` to open the store on disk (migration QA). `LibraryStore.open()` never crashes: a damaged store is moved to `Corrupt-<date>/` and rebuilt from the image files (each carries an XMP tag with its operation and AI marking); a full disk falls back to an in-memory library. DEBUG: `-GLLibraryFault corrupt|diskFull`. Files use `completeFileProtectionUnlessOpen` (on the Mac `…UntilFirstUserAuthentication`: FileVault covers the disk, and a locked screen would leave tiles blank); thumbnails retry when the app becomes active. One window only (`UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes = false`).
- **Ads** (`Services/Ads/`, `Features/Paywall/RewardedAdCard.swift`, AppCore `Ads.swift`, backend `functions/ad-reward`): Google Mobile Ads + UMP for free members 13+ on iPhone and iPad only. The SPM products carry `platformFilters = (ios, )` and every Google call sits behind `#if canImport(GoogleMobileAds) && !targetEnvironment(macCatalyst)` (`GoogleAdProvider`; the Mac gets `NoAdProvider`). `AdCoordinator` (`app.ads`) decides everything and nothing loads for Pro, under 13 (`AgeDefaults.adAudience` is nil, the SDK never starts), on the Mac, in preview builds or offline. The SDK starts on first use, never at launch. Every request is non-personalised (`publisherPrivacyPersonalizationState = .disabled`, `gad_rdp`, first-party ad id off), with `ageRestrictedTreatment = .child` (what TFUA maps to) and a G rating for teens and unknown ages, PG for adults; never request ATT or add `NSUserTrackingUsageDescription`. **Rewarded** ("Watch a short ad · +1 credit", ≤ 3 a day): only for a free member with AI consent and a session who is out of credits (paywall opened by `out_of_credits`, credits sheet), never in `OutOfCreditsSheet` (Pro). Tap → UMP consent (Google's form only for adults in the EEA/UK/CH, only after the tap) → load → `POST /ad-reward` token → the ad with the token as SSV custom data (never the user id) → poll `GET /ad-reward?token=` until `granted` → toast and the sheet closes; the credit comes only from AdMob's signed callback (`_shared/admob.ts` verifies it; `redeem_ad_reward` grants once, caps in SQL). **Interstitial**: `app.ads.noteFinishedExport()` after a finished save or share (editor, Create results, collage, video, share composer) and `naturalBreak()` when a cover or the composer closes (`presentationEnded()`); shown only at a natural break (`acceptsCommands`, tour seen, nothing presented), within `InterstitialPacing` (24 h grace, ≥ 3rd save, 1 per 4 min, 3 a day, 4 min after a rewarded ad), loaded quietly after a save and skipped when not ready; where Google's form is needed and unanswered there's none. Settings › Ad privacy choices appears when UMP says privacy options are required. Config: `AppConfig.json` → `ads` (`appID` must equal Info.plist's `GADApplicationIdentifier` from `GL_ADMOB_APP_ID` in `Release.xcconfig`; empty `interstitialUnitID` = no interstitials); DEBUG builds always use Google's test app and units. DEBUG: `-GLAdRewardState available|loading|pending|capped|offline|nofill|closed|delayed` pins the card (with `paywall -GLPaywallSource out_of_credits` or `home -GLCreditsSheet YES`), `-GLAdPacing off` lets an interstitial follow every save.
- **Offline:** `AppModel.isCloudOffline` (NWPathMonitor; never in demo mode) shows a note on Home and Create and stops cloud actions before any overlay. Transport errors map through `NetworkFailure`. DEBUG: `-GLOffline YES` with a screenshot scene.
- **Shared data with widgets:** App Group `group.app.glowlit.shared`; deep links `glowlit://…` (`GlowDeepLink` in AppCore). `AppModel.handle(_:)` ignores a link while a full-screen cover is open (`isFullScreenCoverPresented`: editor, video editor, Retro Cam, collage), so nothing opens behind it and unsaved work stays.
- **Siri, Shortcuts and tips:** App Intents in `App/Intents/GlowIntents.swift`: *Apply a Retro Effect* (an image file in, the edited JPEG out, on the device and in the background, so it works in automations) and *Open Retro Cam*, *Start Photo Booth*, *Make a Collage*, *Edit a Photo*, which open the app through `AppModel.handle(_:)` like deep links (the editor's own age rules keep under-13s on the free tools). App Shortcut phrases live in `Resources/AppShortcuts.xcstrings`, which Xcode fills from `GlowShortcuts` (like the other catalogs, on `-exportLocalizations`): one string set per shortcut, any number of phrases per language, each with `${applicationName}` exactly once. Intents reach the model through `GlowIntents.app`, set in the app's initializer (not `@Dependency`, which only works inside the system's perform flow). Controls run in the widget extension and can't run app-target intents, so the Retro Cam and Photo Booth controls open the same `glowlit://camera(/booth)` links. TipKit (`Features/Tips/FeatureTips.swift`): tips sit inline in the screen (`.tipSlot(_:)`, never popovers that could block a sheet), one a day, one per screen, never during the tour, each shown once and dropped when she uses the feature; Settings › Show me around brings them back (new tip ids: TipKit can't reset its store while running) and Delete my data empties the store at the next launch. DEBUG: scene `tips`, `-GLTip <name>` with another scene, and `-GLIntent <name>` performs an intent.
- **Retro Cam** (`Features/Camera`, `Services/Camera`): an AVCaptureSession on its own queue; video frames go as CIImages through `RetroEffect.grade` into a CAMetalLayer with the shared, Metal-backed `ImageFilters.context` (never a UIImage per frame); stills come from AVCapturePhotoOutput and get the full `apply`. Booth timing and print layout are in AppCore (`PhotoBooth`, `BoothLayout`), prints in `BoothPrint`. Glamour (soft focus) and Kira Kira aren't offered live. The simulator has no camera: DEBUG builds feed demo art instead (`RetroCamFixture`, also with `-GLCameraFixture YES`).
- **Layout:** universal; use `.readableWidth()` for wide screens and `pageSheetSizing()` for big sheets; Liquid Glass only behind `#if compiler(>=6.2)` + `#available(iOS 26, *)`. The editor's tool bar (`EditorToolBar`) never shrinks labels: one row when every tool fits (iPad, Mac), a scrolling row on iPhone. Brush canvases (Erase, Cut out's refine brush) use `BrushCanvas`: one finger paints, two fingers pan and pinch. On the Mac the tabs sit in the title bar, so full-window covers (editor, video editor, Retro Cam, collage) hide them with `hidesMacTabs`.
- **Mac (Mac Catalyst):** both targets set `SUPPORTS_MACCATALYST` and keep `TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1,2` ("Scale Interface to Match iPad", macOS 14+) and the iOS bundle ids (`DERIVE_MACCATALYST_PRODUCT_BUNDLE_IDENTIFIER = NO`, so purchases are universal). macOS signs with `Config/*-macOS.entitlements` (App Sandbox, network client, camera, user-selected files, Photos library, App Group, Sign in with Apple for the app) through `CODE_SIGN_ENTITLEMENTS[sdk=macosx*]`. `App/MacSupport.swift` has the menu bar (`GlowlitCommands`, attached on the Mac only), the window setup (no title text, 900×640 minimum), photo drag & drop and Mac-only shortcuts; share sheets go through `.shareSheet(item:)`, a popover on the Mac. Not on the Mac: the Live Activity (ActivityKit is unavailable there, so a finished job uses the opt-in notification), Instagram Stories (the share menu takes its place) and ads (Google Mobile Ads has no Mac Catalyst build: platform filter plus `canImport` guards); the Control Center controls reach the Mac build only with the Xcode 26 SDK. Use `#if targetEnvironment(macCatalyst)` for APIs, but `Platform.isMac` at runtime for Mac wording ("on your Mac", "System Settings"), so `-exportLocalizations`, which builds for iOS, still extracts it.

## 5. Rules

- **Never commit or print secrets.** AI keys live in Supabase Edge Function secrets or the git-ignored `ios/Config/Secrets.xcconfig`. Public-by-design values (Supabase anon key, RevenueCat `appl_…` key) may go in `AppConfig.json`.
- Don't commit `.claude/`. Don't add personal email addresses to public files.
- **Teen safety:** no sexual, undressing, revealing-clothing, humiliating or body/face/skin-reshaping features or prompts. The prompt policy lives in `ContentPolicy.swift` (terms per category, all app languages, subject-bound so "slim gold necklace" still works); after changing it run `scripts/export-catalog.sh`, which writes `_shared/policy-terms.json` for `policy.ts`, and add cases to `_shared/policy-vectors.json` (run by both `swift test` and `deno test`). Every Magic Edit ends with `PromptBuilder.editGuard` (the server appends it too). Template prompts keep identity, skin tone and body unchanged and outfits age-appropriate.
- **Ads stay rare, optional and non-personalised:** never under 13, for Pro or on the Mac; never ATT or the IDFA; consent forms only after she asks for an ad; no new ad placements (banners, app-open ads, ads inside the editor or before a result is safe) without an owner decision and updated legal texts. Ad credits come only from the server's verified AdMob callback, never from the app.
- **Honest copy:** claims in the app, website, App Store text and legal documents must match the code (example: there is no remote template catalog, so never promise new looks without an app update). Don't name competitors in user-facing copy.
- **Performance** (numbers in `docs/04-architecture.md` › Performance): render with the shared `ImageFilters.context` (or `ImageFilters.measuringContext` for unmanaged pixel measuring), never a new `CIContext`; decode photos with `ImageUtilities.decodedImage` / `ImageStore.decoded` / `ImageStore.downsampled` (ImageIO, one plain copy), never `UIImage(data:)` for pictures you process; no decoding, resizing, encoding or file I/O on the main actor; draw opaque results with `ImageUtilities.draw(…, opaque: true)` (8-bit, no alpha, so they save as JPEG; only zero-offset shadows there) and keep `UIGraphicsImageRenderer` for transparent art; keep launch work out of `GlowlitApp.init` and the first frame (`AppModel.start()` runs after it). Check a change with `-GLPerfBench <photo>` / `-GLPerfOpen <photo>` (`PerfProbe`).
- **Legal:** edit `legal/` only, then run `python3 scripts/build-legal.py`. Adding a data processor means updating the privacy policies (EN, TR, KVKK) in the same change.
- Match the surrounding code style: SwiftUI + Observation, sparse comments that explain *why*, Blush palette tokens, `Text(styled:)` for headlines with one accent word.
- Commit messages: imperative summary, short body, and the co-author trailer used in this repo's history.
