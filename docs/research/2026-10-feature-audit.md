# Feature audit and three build waves (14 October 2026)

On 13–14 October 2026 we audited Aybuke from seven angles, merged the results into one ranked backlog, and built the top tier in three waves on the same day (merges `91eb39e` to `569f9df`, after `d887f77`). This document records the research behind those decisions: what we looked at, what we found, what we built, what we put off and what we refused to build.

For the current state of the app see [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md); for the remaining work see [06-roadmap.md](../06-roadmap.md). Earlier research lives in [market-research.md](market-research.md), [tech-and-compliance.md](tech-and-compliance.md) and [07-audit-and-differentiators.md](../07-audit-and-differentiators.md).

## 1. How the audit was done

Seven audits ran in parallel. All of them were read-only (nothing was edited or built), and each produced a summary plus items with code evidence (file and line), sources, user value, effort, whether the backend is needed, and teen-safety notes.

| Audit | Scope |
|---|---|
| **Code completeness** | Every Swift file under `ios/App`, `ios/GlowlitWidgets` and AppCore, checked against AGENTS.md, the product spec, the roadmap, App Store metadata, the website, the bundled legal pages and the Edge Functions |
| **UX and accessibility** | Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Voice Control, contrast (WCAG ratios computed from the palette), touch targets, iPad windows, memory, Lock Screen surfaces, localization; thresholds from Apple's accessibility evaluation criteria |
| **Platform features** | Apple frameworks available on iOS 17, 18, 26 and 27 (iOS 27 shipped on 15 Sep 2026) and how Aybuke uses them |
| **Competitors** | About 25 apps and platforms that teens and young women used in 2025–2026; most App Store pages were opened directly on 13–14 Oct 2026 |
| **Trends and charts** | Viral AI photo formats from mid-2025 to Oct 2026, the US Photo & Video top-free chart, aesthetics forecasts |
| **Wellbeing, retention and monetization** | Research on teens, appearance and AI; retention patterns; subscription benchmarks; App Review and EU consumer rules on paywalls |
| **Compliance** | App Review, age-assurance laws, GDPR/KVKK, COPPA, the EU AI Act and Code of Practice, UK and US deepfake laws, country rules |

The seven reports produced about 205 findings. We merged duplicates (the teen-safety gap in the prompt policy, for example, was raised independently by four audits), spot-checked the most important claims in the code, and ranked everything into **66 backlog items in three tiers**:

- **implement-now (26):** worth doing now and possible without the backend. All 26 were built (one of them in part; see §7).
- **later (32):** valuable, but they need the backend, owner or counsel decisions, new targets, or more effort than one round allows (§8).
- **rejected (8):** ideas competitors use or trends invite that break Aybuke's hard rules (§9).

Ranking followed AGENTS.md: teen safety first, then trust-breaking bugs, then compliance, then value that works while the backend is still in demo mode, then robustness and accessibility.

**Limits.** Many trend roundups are vendor or SEO blogs; where we could, we relied on NBC, NPR, TechCrunch, Gulf News, the Korea Herald, InsideAI and Pinterest data. A few competitor claims (Glam AI pricing, some trend lists) rest on third-party coverage. The compliance audit ran out of web-search budget near the end, so some statutory texts (BIPA, UCPD Annex I No. 28, Italy's Law 132/2025 Art. 4(4), Korea PIPA Art. 22-2) were not re-fetched and need counsel's check. We found no evidence of a Turkish Yeşilçam-style AI trend, so we treated that idea as a hypothesis only.

## 2. What the audit found

1. **Teen safety gap in our own code.** Magic Edit and Create blocked only sexual and undressing words. "Make me thinner", "smaller nose", "lighter skin", "flawless skin", "put her in a bikini" and "me hugging <celebrity>" all reached the model, and `PromptBuilder.editInstruction` dropped its identity clause whenever the text contained "face", which is exactly the reshaping case. This is the misuse that led Ofcom to investigate X/Grok in January 2026 ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/01/15/ofcom_grok_probe/), [Simmons & Simmons](https://www.simmons-simmons.com/en/publications/cmkfjc1xl0030v4tklthq0jn3/grok-generative-ai-and-the-uk-online-safety-act)), and xAI is our default provider.
2. **Bugs that break trust:** Save ignored an effect that hadn't been applied; a failed "Retry free" deleted the result she paid for; the watermark and AI label were added to on-device edits and dropped from re-edited AI results; Ask to Buy failed silently; Pro users who ran out were told credits "refill at midnight".
3. **Honest-copy breaks:** "fresh trends every week", "Fresh looks added regularly", "higher resolution" for Pro (both tiers render at 1k) and a price comparison with no source.
4. **Compliance:** a one-sentence age gate; an anonymous account and RevenueCat identity created at first launch, before any age screen or consent; an automatic paywall after onboarding for every age; notifications that could fire at night; Lock Screen surfaces showing her prompt and latest selfie; "Help me pick" letting friends vote on her real face against her AI glow-up.
5. **Value without the backend:** no crop, rotate or zoom; no way to hide other people's faces; cut-outs with nothing to put behind them; seasonal looks shown all year; no real first success in onboarding; Apple's free on-device AI unused.
6. **Robustness and accessibility:** full-size decoding of 48 MP photos, 20 full bitmaps of undo, results lost when the phone locked, a SwiftData failure that crashed every launch, no offline detection, iPad windows that mirrored each other, a consent sheet that couldn't scroll at large text sizes, a video editor VoiceOver couldn't operate, and no plural forms ("1 credits left").

### Why some popular features stay out

Competitors earn much of their money from slimming, reshaping, skin smoothing and lightening. Aybuke's rule is the opposite: every look keeps her face, skin tone, skin texture, body and age. That rule ruled out the beauty-retouch tools listed above. It also shaped what we adopted:

- **Hide faces** protects friends' consent and her own privacy, so it was adopted at once (it also hides the same faces in the Before image, so Before/After, Photo strip and Which vibe? never reveal them).
- **Stickers** are made only from her own cut-outs; there is no sticker marketplace and no "rating" stickers.
- **Backdrops** are bundled colours, gradients, a blur of the original or her own photo; no location or school backdrops that could show where she is.
- **Image Playground** runs with personalization off (so the sheet cannot pick other people from her library), no source image and Apple's own styles only; ChatGPT styles would add an undisclosed processor.
- **Upscaling**, when it comes, must use plain super-resolution, not a face-restoration model such as GFPGAN, which invents facial detail.

## 5. Teen wellbeing, retention and monetization

### What the research says

- **Appearance and AI.** The APA's June 2025 advisory asks for protected likeness, age-appropriate defaults and less persuasive design ([APA](https://www.apa.org/news/press/releases/2025/06/protect-adolescent-ai-users)). The Children's Commissioner for England reported in February 2026 that 78% of 13–17s say appearance-changing products hurt self-esteem ([Children's Commissioner](https://www.childrenscommissioner.gov.uk/news-and-blogs/press-notice-appearance-changing-products-like-ozempic-steroids-and-botox-advertised-to-children-on-social-media-platforms-childrens-commissioner-calls-for-tighte)). A 2025 Spanish survey links editing facial features with filters to lower satisfaction ([UNIR](https://reunir.unir.net/items/f3d5462e-1ac8-4950-bd8f-71fbc547c9cb/full)). Girlguiding found 54% of girls wish they looked like their filtered selves ([Girlguiding](https://www.girlguiding.org.uk/about-us/press-releases/gas-teaser-24)).
- **Labels alone don't help.** "This image is edited" disclaimers don't reduce body dissatisfaction ([York University](https://news.yorku.ca/2019/11/28/disclaimers-on-retouched-photos-dont-solve-problem-of-negative-body-image/), [The Learning Scientists](https://www.learningscientists.org/blog/2025/6/26)). AI image generators default to the thin, fit ideal ([University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca/news/u-t-study-asks-ai-generate-male-and-female-body-images-predictable-results)), while AI images of diverse bodies can improve body image ([Gelişim University study](https://avesis.gelisim.edu.tr/yayin/8f586c52-8435-4d5d-930d-eea02549aec1/the-effects-of-exposure-to-imagery-created-with-generative-artificial-intelligence-gen-ai-on-young-womens-body-image-do-image-type-and-disclosure-matter), [Dove's AI pledge](https://reviewed.com/beauty/news/dove-real-beauty-campaign-ai-pledge)).
- **Nudges and minors.** The UK ICO Children's Code standard 13 covers nudge techniques ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/13-nudge-techniques/)). The EU DSA Art. 28 guidelines (July 2025) say AI features should be opt-in and minors shouldn't be enticed to use them ([Freshfields](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/technology-quotient/dsa-decoded-6-the-european-commission-finalises-guidelines-on-the-protection-of-102kv4s)). EU consumer authorities treated "Buy now" pop-ups aimed at children as direct exhortation in the Star Stable action ([BDK Advokati](https://bdkadvokati.com/eu-consumer-protection-authorities-address-harmful-commercial-practices-affecting-children-in-gaming)). Snapchat streaks are linked to problematic phone use ([ASU](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/snapchat-streakshow-are-these-forms-of-gamified-interactions-asso)).
- **Retention.** 91% of Photo & Video trial starts happen on Day 0, and PhotoRoom's pattern puts a required first success before the paywall ([RevenueCat: paywall placement](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/paywall-placement/), [Adapty Photo & Video benchmarks](https://adapty.io/blog/photo-video-app-subscription-benchmarks/), [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/of-trials-start-on-day-0-dont-waste-your-shot)).
- **Monetization.** $39.99 a year matches RevenueCat's 2026 North-America yearly median and $5.99 a month is under the $9.99 median, so prices stay ([RevenueCat SOSA 2026](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps)). Photo & Video has the highest trial refund rate (6.4%) and the lowest trial-to-paid rate (22.2%). Apple rejected a "Try for Free" CTA under 3.1.2(c) in August 2026 when the billed price wasn't next to it ([Apple forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/841626), [RevenueCat on toggle paywalls](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/rip-toggle-paywall)). The EU consumer network's virtual-currency principles ask for prices in real money next to credits ([Baker McKenzie](https://connectontech.bakermckenzie.com/european-consumer-protection-network-issues-new-key-principles-on-in-game-virtual-currencies-impact-for-gaming-and-gambling-entities-in-belgium-the-eu-and-beyond/), [Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/articles/qas-on-the-eu-consumer-protection-authorities-joint-guidance-paper/)).

### Product decisions they drove

| Research | Decision (built) |
|---|---|
| Appearance-changing edits hurt teens | The prompt policy blocks reshaping, skin lightening and smoothing, revealing clothing and humiliation in all 12 app languages; every Magic Edit ends with an edit guard that keeps faces, bodies, skin tone, skin texture and age (added by the app and again by the server). The server's Enhance prompt now keeps real skin texture |
| Comparison and rating harm | "Help me pick" became **Which vibe?** and only pairs two variations of one creation, never her original against an edit. **Best shot** lost its crown and rank numbers; the top photo gets a "Best light & focus" chip |
| Labels don't fix body image | A small **Feel good** page in Settings points to someone she trusts and findahelpline.com, with no tracking. **Caption ideas** talk about the vibe, never about looks |
| Ideas should be safe by default | **Ideas for this photo** never suggests beauty looks or Glamour '92 for photos of people; it keeps to hair colour, backgrounds, light, props and styles |
| Nudges and minors | Under 18 never sees the paywall by itself; the Friday look and trend widget use only free, non-makeup looks unless she has Pro; notifications never quote her prompt |
| First success before the paywall | Onboarding ends with Digicam '04 on her own photo; Keep editing opens it in the editor, Save keeps it; the adult soft paywall waits for that success, and the tour follows |
| 3.1.2(c) and honest pricing | The billed terms sit at button size beside a neutral CTA; the unsupported price comparison is gone; Ask to Buy shows a neutral "Waiting for approval" state |
| Real-money value of credits | Credit packs show a per-credit price; Pro members who run out see the next monthly refill and the packs instead of "refills at midnight" |
| Retention without pressure | No streaks, feeds or likes. A private weekly theme, recaps, TipKit, App Shortcuts and extensions are **later** |

Not yet taken up: countering the AI "fit ideal" when Create prompts depict people (a prompt change worth testing with real results once the backend is live).
