## GDPR / privacy

- **Biometrics:** face photos are not biometric data under Art. 9 unless they are processed for identification. **Never** build face embeddings, matching or "find this person" features.
- **Roles:** we are the controller; xAI (the default AI provider), Anthropic (the independent safety check), Supabase, RevenueCat, and fal.ai and its model vendors where used, are processors. Anthropic processes the photos and results it checks on our instructions, but to enforce its own Usage Policy and meet its legal duties (including reporting child sexual abuse material to the authorities) it may keep flagged content up to 2 years and acts as an independent controller for that (Privacy Policy §11, §12; KVKK notice §5). Google (AdMob) serves and confirms ads and acts partly as an independent controller (Privacy Policy §5, §11, §12). ✅ Sub-processor list in Privacy Policy §12 (xAI, Anthropic, RevenueCat and Google AdMob included). ⏳ Sign DPAs (Anthropic's included) and accept Google's ads data protection terms in AdMob; ⏳ appoint an Art. 27 representative if there is no EU establishment; ⏳ counsel to confirm the KVKK art. 9 basis for the SDK's transfers to Google.
- **Contracts and transfers for Anthropic and xAI (owner, before launch):** the texts name the safeguards we rely on (Privacy Policy §13, KVKK notice §5), but the DPAs and the KVKK standard contracts with Anthropic and xAI may not be signed yet; until they are, that wording isn't backed. The wording stays as it is (no safeguard was added to it). ⏳ Sign Anthropic's DPA and xAI's. ⏳ Settle the US transfer basis for each (Data Privacy Framework certification, or the SCCs with the UK Addendum and the Swiss adaptations). ⏳ Sign the KVKK Board's standard contract with each and notify the KVKK Authority within 5 business days of signing. ⏳ Have counsel confirm the privacy texts against what was signed. ⏳ Anthropic's guidance for products used by minors: confirm with counsel and Anthropic whether it applies to a backend classifier that never talks to the user. Tracked in `docs/SAHIP-YAPILACAKLAR.md` 6.3.
- **Lawful basis:** contract, for performing the requested edit, plus the explicit consent screen. ⏳ Run a DPIA.

**Retention, as implemented**

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Inputs | Never stored by us; passed straight to the AI provider (xAI by default). With xAI Zero Data Retention on (✅ Active for the team since 15 October 2026), xAI deletes requests and results within an hour and keeps no copies. The photo of an edit also goes to Anthropic for the safety check first: Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, may keep content its systems flag for a usage-policy violation up to 2 years, and doesn't train on API data by default |
| Outputs | xAI: returned inline (base64) and kept on the job row only until the app confirms the download, then cleared; `sweep_jobs` clears any left after 1 hour. fal: expire on the fal CDN after 1 hour; fal request history is off. Every result is also sent to Anthropic for the safety check, with the retention above |
| Job rows | Deleted after 30 days (`sweep_jobs`) |
| Library | On the device only |
| Videos and on-device edits | Never leave the device |
| Purchase analytics | Purchase records, our account ID and basic device and app information, held by RevenueCat as our processor; no photos, prompts or results |
| Account, ledger, entitlements | Deleted, with all rows, on account deletion (cascade) |
| Ad rewards | Reward tokens: valid 30 minutes, deleted a day after expiry; reward records: 30 days (`sweep_ad_rewards`); both cascade on account deletion. Ad pacing counts stay on the device |

## United States

- **California AI Transparency Act** (in force since 2 Aug 2026; applies above 1M monthly users) and **Washington ESHB 1170** (from 1 Feb 2027) require embedded provenance, and accept C2PA. ⏳ Add C2PA before reaching that scale.
- **TAKE IT DOWN Act:** applies to platforms that host user content publicly. Aybuke has no public sharing; adding a feed would trigger a 48-hour removal process.
- **Age assurance:** Texas SB 2420 (new Texas Apple Accounts from 4 Jun 2026, https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=sg176nne), Utah from 6 May 2026 and Louisiana from 1 Jul 2026 (https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=f5zj08ey), Brazil's ECA Digital (no self-declaration). ✅ Declared Age Range API (`com.apple.developer.declared-age-range`), with the gates 13, 16 and 18; where Apple says it's required (`requiredRegulatoryFeatures` / `isEligibleForAgeFeatures`), declining keeps on-device-only mode with a retry; in Brazil a self-declared age gets only the protective defaults (counsel to confirm). ⏳ Later (backend): PermissionKit significant-change approvals and App Store Server `RESCIND_CONSENT` handling; `AppStore.ageRatingCode`; mirroring the band to the widgets. ⏳ California AB 1043 and Alabama dates: cite primary sources before relying on them.
- **BIPA and right of publicity:** no face templates; no celebrity or "real person" templates.
