# Sample photos for marketing captures

These pictures fill the app's DEBUG screenshot scenes when they're launched
with `-GLSamplePhotos <path to this folder>` (see `ScreenshotFixtures` in
`ios/App/App/ScreenshotScenes.swift`), so the App Store screenshots show real-
looking photos instead of the drawn fixtures. They never ship in the app.

- **Made with AI for Aybuke** (xAI Grok Imagine, October 2026), through a
  temporary server function that used the project's own key; nothing here
  comes from a stock site, so there are no third-party licences or model
  releases to track.
- **Every person is fictional and an adult** (described as in their twenties).
  Don't replace them with photos of real people, and never with minors.
- The edits (`portrait-a-pink-hair`, `portrait-a-soft-glam`,
  `portrait-b-golden`, `lake-golden`) were made from the matching originals
  with the same kind of instruction the app sends for "Give me pink hair",
  Makeup Studio's Soft Glam and "Golden hour light".
