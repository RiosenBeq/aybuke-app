# Social media kit

Everything you need to launch and run Glowlit's social channels, in English and Turkish. Glowlit is the AI photo editor **made for girls who glow**: say what to change, tap a trend, share the glow-up.

> **Ground rules for every post**
> - Celebrate creativity, never "fix" anyone. No body-shaming, no "flawless skin" or "slimmer" promises, no comments on how people look.
> - Never sexualise anyone, and never feature minors in paid or creator content.
> - Label AI content: switch on Instagram's "AI info" and TikTok's "AI-generated content" toggle, and keep the "made with AI" wording in captions.
> - Paid partnerships are always disclosed (#ad / #reklam plus the platform's branded-content tool).
> - No third-party brands or characters (no doll, toy, film or celebrity names) in posts, templates or hashtags.

## 1. Positioning, pillars and voice

**Positioning (EN).** For girls and young women who live on Instagram and TikTok, Glowlit is the AI photo editor that turns a sentence or a tap into a glow-up that still looks like you, with honest pricing and photos that stay private.

**Konumlandırma (TR).** Instagram ve TikTok'ta yaşayan kızlar ve genç kadınlar için Glowlit, bir cümleyi ya da tek bir dokunuşu hâlâ sana benzeyen bir glow-up'a dönüştüren, adil fiyatlı ve fotoğrafları gizli tutan yapay zekâlı fotoğraf editörüdür.

**Three pillars**

1. **Say it, see it** (Magic Edit): the magic of typing "give me pink hair" and watching it happen.
2. **Trends first** (one-tap looks): Doll Box, Y2K Digicam, Ballet Core, Fairy Princess and more, one tap each.
3. **Glow safely** (privacy and fair pricing): consent before cloud AI, never used to train AI, no weekly plan, failed edits refunded.

**Voice: do**

- Warm, confident, playful, a bit dreamy: "Main-character energy, unlocked ✨"
- Short sentences, one emoji per line at most, sentence case.
- Talk about the edit, the vibe and the creativity, not the person's body or face.
- Honest about what's free and what's Pro.

**Voice: don't**

- "Fix your face", "flawless", "perfect skin", "slimmer", "prettier".
- Fake urgency ("only today!"), fake scarcity, or claims we can't prove ("the #1 app").
- Slang that mocks anyone or excludes anyone.

**Ses tonu (TR):** sıcak, kendinden emin, eğlenceli ve biraz rüya gibi. Düzenlemeyi, havayı ve yaratıcılığı konuş; kimsenin bedeni veya yüzü hakkında yorum yapma. "Kusursuz cilt", "incelt", "güzelleştir" gibi ifadeler yok.

## 2. Visual direction

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Blush | `#F7C1D9` | Hero colour: big flat fields, stickers, highlights |
| Petal | `#F8E0EA` | Light pink backgrounds |
| Ink | `#121212` | Bold headlines, buttons, outlines |
| Charcoal | `#24272A` | Dark sections and night-mode posts |
| Paper | `#F8F8F6` | Neutral cards |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Photo frames, sparkles |

- **Signature look:** bold ink headline on blush, with one word in an elegant italic serif ("Make it *glow.*"). Photos sit in white rounded frames with a soft shadow.
- **Hero device:** the before/after split with the compare handle.
- **Motifs:** sparkles ✦ and small hearts, used sparingly.
- **Type:** headlines in the app's display font (Poppins or Bricolage Grotesque), accents in New York Italic or Playfair Display Italic, body in SF Pro or Inter.
- **Formats:** 1080×1350 for feed posts and carousels, 1080×1920 for Stories and Reels covers (keep text out of the top and bottom 250 px).
- Ready-made templates are in `docs/marketing/social/` (generated by `scripts/marketing-images.py`).

## 6. Short-form video scripts (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)

Each one is 7–20 s. Hook in the first 1.5 s, text on screen in the first frame, captions on.

1. **"Say it" reveal (8 s).** Shot: phone screen recording. Text: "I typed 'give me pink hair'…". VO EN: "I just typed it… and watch." VO TR: "Sadece yazdım… izle." Cut on the beat to the slider swipe from before to after.
2. **Doll Box unboxing (12 s).** Text: "Turning myself into a collectible doll 🎀". Hand taps the template; zoom into the result; the accessories pop in one by one on the beat.
3. **POV: digicam (9 s).** Text: "POV: you found your 2004 digital camera". Normal selfie → Y2K Digicam result with flash sound effect.
4. **Restore grandma's photo (18 s).** Text: "Restoring my grandma's photo from 1972". Slow reveal with the slider; end card "Memories deserve a glow". VO TR: "Babaannemin 1972'den kalma fotoğrafını onardım."
5. **Tell me without telling me (10 s).** Text: "Tell me you're a ballet girl without telling me". Ballet Core result, satin bow close-up.
6. **3 looks, 1 selfie (15 s).** Text: "Same selfie, 3 aesthetics". Soft Glam → Clean Girl → Fairy Princess, each on a beat drop.
7. **Free trick (7 s).** Text: "Free background remover, no upload". Tap Remove BG; checkerboard appears; paste onto a pink story card.
8. **Prompt challenge (14 s).** Text: "Comment a prompt, I'll create it". Read a comment, type it into Create, show the result.
9. **Café date in 1 tap (9 s).** Text: "When the café is closed but the aesthetic isn't". Café Date result with soft lo-fi audio.
10. **Privacy in 10 s (10 s).** Text: "What happens to your photo?". Three quick cards: "Only after you say yes", "Never trains AI", "Deleted within an hour".

Production notes: record in the app's light (pink) mode, use trending audio from the platform's commercial music library for brand accounts, and add the AI label.

## 9. UGC, challenges and referral ideas

- **#GlowlitChallenge:** one trend per week (Pink Hair, Ballet Core, Doll Box, Bestie Polaroid). Feature favourites only with written permission in DMs.
- **Template of the week:** a Monday post with a new or trending template, and a Friday recap of community creations.
- **Share cards:** the in-app share sheet adds "Made with Glowlit" on free exports, so every share is a quiet referral.
- **No incentives for ratings:** App Store rules forbid rewarding ratings or reviews. Never offer credits for reviews, and don't make sharing a condition for using any feature.
- **Seasonal moments:** Valentine's Day (bestie looks), Mother's Day (photo restoration), graduation (headshots), Halloween (spooky looks), New Year (birthday and party glam).
